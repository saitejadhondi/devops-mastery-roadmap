# Chapter 3: Monitoring vs Logging vs Observability

Many beginners confuse these.

---

# Monitoring

Answers:

```text
IS SOMETHING WRONG?
```

---

Example:

```text
CPU = 95%
```

Problem detected.

---

# Logging

Answers:

```text
WHAT WENT WRONG?
```

---

Example:

```text
Database Connection Timeout
```

Cause identified.

---

# Observability

Answers:

```text
WHY DID IT GO WRONG?
```

---

Provides complete understanding.

---

Simple Mental Model:

```text
Monitoring
      ↓
Problem Found

Logging
      ↓
Problem Located

Observability
      ↓
Problem Understood
```

# 🔥 Interview Questions

### What Is Monitoring?

Continuous observation of systems and applications to detect problems and ensure reliability.

---

### Difference Between Monitoring And Observability?

Monitoring detects known issues.

Observability helps understand unknown issues.

---

### What Are Metrics?

Numerical measurements describing system and application behavior.

---

### What Are Logs?

Recorded events generated by applications and infrastructure.

---

### What Are Traces?

Records showing how requests travel through distributed systems.

---

### What Is Prometheus?

An open-source monitoring system used for collecting and storing metrics.

---

### What Is Grafana?

A dashboard and visualization platform for monitoring data.

---

### What Is ELK Stack?

A centralized logging solution consisting of Elasticsearch, Logstash, and Kibana.

---

### What Is AlertManager?

A component that sends notifications when monitoring rules are violated.

---

### What Is The Difference Between SLA, SLO, And SLI?

SLI = Measurement

SLO = Target

SLA = Customer Commitment

---

# One-Line Summary

Monitoring tells you that something is wrong, logging tells you what went wrong, and observability helps you understand why it went wrong so you can fix it quickly.
